# SmartSave – FinTech Business Analysis Case Study

## Project Overview

SmartSave is a collaborative Business Analysis case study for a fintech solution designed to help young professionals build healthier financial habits through automated savings, intelligent budgeting, AI-powered financial insights, and personalized financial goal tracking.

The project involved analyzing a business problem, eliciting and documenting business requirements, modeling current and future business processes, and proposing a digital solution that improves users' financial wellness.

**Project Type:** Team Business Analysis Case Study

---

## Business Problem

Young professionals often struggle to save consistently due to:

- Limited visibility into their spending
- Manual and inconsistent budgeting practices
- Lack of personalized financial guidance
- Low engagement with existing financial tools

SmartSave addresses these challenges by providing an intelligent, automated, and user-friendly financial management experience.

---

## My Role

This project was completed collaboratively as part of a Business Analysis team.

### My primary responsibilities included:

- Conducting the **Gap Analysis** to identify business challenges and improvement opportunities.
- Developing the **As-Is Process Model** to document the current-state customer journey and operational pain points.
- Designing the **To-Be Process Model** to illustrate the future-state solution and proposed business improvements.
- Contributing to the **Business Requirements Document (BRD)** by documenting assigned business requirements and collaborating with teammates to ensure consistency across the final deliverable.
