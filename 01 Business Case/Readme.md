# Business Case

## Overview

The Business Case evaluates the viability of SmartSave by defining the business problem, proposed solution, expected benefits, costs, risks, and strategic value.

## Purpose

The objective of this document is to justify investment in SmartSave by demonstrating how the proposed solution addresses user pain points while supporting business objectives.

## Deliverables

- Executive Summary
- Problem Statement
- Business Goals and Objectives
- Scope
- Stakeholders
- Cost & Benefits Analysis
- Risk Analysis
- Timeline
- Recommendations
